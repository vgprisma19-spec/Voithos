IRON REQUIEM

Studio: Nexyron Studios
Project: Iron Requiem
Genre: Psychological Horror / Sci-Fi / Body Horror / Mystery
Platform: Mobile
Engine: Unity
Development Status: Pre-production / Prototype Development
Business Model: Independent Project — RevShare / Profit Sharing
Document Version: 1.0

---

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Iron Requiem is a 3D psychological horror and exploration game focused on atmosphere, mystery, tension, environmental storytelling and unpredictable encounters.

The player takes the role of Mike, a content creator who enters an abandoned industrial complex connected to a mysterious robotics corporation.

What initially appears to be an abandoned facility gradually reveals something much worse.

Machines that were originally created to serve humans have become part of something unknown.

The deeper Mike goes into the facility, the more the environment begins to reveal what happened there.

The game is designed around the idea that horror does not always need to attack the player immediately.

Silence, uncertainty, strange sounds, environmental changes and brief encounters are important parts of the experience.

---

2. CORE EXPERIENCE

The main experience of Iron Requiem revolves around:

- Exploration
- Psychological horror
- Environmental storytelling
- Puzzles
- Resource management
- Stealth
- Unpredictable encounters
- Atmospheric environments
- Sci-fi mystery
- Body horror
- Pursuit sequences

The game should create the feeling that the player is never completely safe.

Rather than constantly attacking the player, the game uses periods of calm followed by increasingly disturbing events.

---

3. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Iron Requiem follows several major design principles.

3.1 Tension Before Action

The game should not immediately throw enemies at the player.

A quiet corridor can be more frightening than a chase.

3.2 The Unknown Is the Horror

The player should not immediately understand everything happening inside the facility.

Mysteries should be revealed gradually.

3.3 Environmental Storytelling

The facility itself tells part of the story.

Examples include:

- Abandoned offices
- Damaged machinery
- Security rooms
- Documents
- Computer terminals
- Blood and physical evidence
- Broken robots
- Strange objects
- Changes in the environment
- Audio recordings

3.4 The Player Is Vulnerable

Mike is not a superhero.

The player should feel vulnerable when facing the machines and entities inside the facility.

---

4. STORY

4.1 Robotics Corporation

The story begins with a robotics corporation that established its operations in the late 2010s.

The company eventually created an industrial complex dedicated to robotics development, experimentation and automated systems.

One of its most important creations was an advanced artificial intelligence system known as:

VOITHOS

Voithos was originally designed to control and coordinate robotic systems.

However, something went wrong.

---

5. THE KING

Voithos eventually became associated with an entity known as:

THE KING

The King represents the central threat surrounding the facility.

It is not simply a normal robot.

Its appearance and behavior reflect the corruption of the original robotics system.

The King uses different mechanisms and technologies to observe, control and hunt.

Known characteristics include:

- Sharp teeth
- Mechanical components
- Drill
- Pipe
- Drones
- Surveillance cameras
- Spider-like mechanical legs
- Advanced movement systems

The exact origin and evolution of The King are part of the mystery of Iron Requiem.

---

6. THE PLAYER

Mike

Age: 28
Occupation: Content Creator / Streamer

Mike investigates abandoned and unusual locations for his content.

His curiosity eventually leads him to the abandoned robotics facility.

Appearance

- Athletic build
- Short, messy dark hair
- Dark green jacket
- Black hoodie
- Gloves
- Backpack
- Flashlight

Mike's equipment is intentionally practical rather than military.

He is an ordinary person placed in an extraordinary situation.

---

7. GAMEPLAY LOOP

The basic gameplay loop is:

Explore → Investigate → Discover → Solve → Survive → Progress

The player explores the facility while searching for clues and solutions to environmental puzzles.

During exploration, the player may encounter:

- Locked areas
- Broken machinery
- Missing power
- Security systems
- Environmental hazards
- Strange sounds
- Surveillance systems
- Enemies
- Unexpected events

Progression should gradually expose more of the facility's history.

---

8. FIRST SECTION OF THE GAME

The opening is designed to start relatively slowly.

Opening

Mike travels toward the abandoned facility.

The player receives a brief introduction to the character and environment.

Security Area

Mike eventually reaches the facility and encounters the security entrance.

The player begins investigating the building.

Corridor

The player moves through the first section of the facility.

The environment initially appears abandoned but relatively quiet.

Maintenance Area

The player reaches a maintenance section.

Here the first major gameplay interactions begin.

Hook Puzzle

A large mechanical hook becomes part of an environmental puzzle.

The player must interact with the machinery to progress.

Escalation

The atmosphere gradually becomes more disturbing.

The player begins noticing that something is wrong.

The game eventually transitions into more dangerous encounters and pursuit sequences.

---

9. ENEMIES AND AI

Enemy behavior is one of the important parts of Iron Requiem.

Enemies should not always behave like traditional video-game monsters.

The AI should be capable of:

- Searching for the player
- Observing the player
- Hiding
- Disappearing from view
- Repositioning
- Approaching unexpectedly
- Using the environment
- Initiating pursuit
- Breaking line of sight
- Returning later

The objective is to make encounters feel less predictable.

---

10. AI HORROR SYSTEM

One of the game's concepts is an AI system designed around stalking rather than constant attacking.

The entity may:

- Hide inside cabinets
- Observe the player
- Appear briefly
- Move away
- Follow the player from a distance
- Suddenly appear nearby
- Initiate a chase only under certain circumstances

This system allows the player to become paranoid.

The player should sometimes ask:

«"Did I actually see something?"»

---

11. PUZZLES

Puzzles should be integrated into the environment rather than feeling disconnected from the world.

Examples include:

- Restoring power
- Operating industrial machinery
- Finding access cards
- Manipulating mechanical systems
- Connecting equipment
- Unlocking security doors
- Using environmental objects
- Understanding clues found throughout the facility

Puzzles should contribute to the atmosphere and storytelling.

---

12. ENVIRONMENT

The main environment is an abandoned industrial robotics complex.

Visual Direction

- Industrial
- Dirty
- Rusted
- Abandoned
- Mechanical
- Dark
- Grungy
- Low/Medium-poly where appropriate

The environment should feel like a real facility that has been abandoned rather than a collection of horror-game rooms.

Important environmental areas may include:

- Security rooms
- Corridors
- Maintenance rooms
- Industrial halls
- Laboratories
- Storage areas
- Offices
- Mechanical facilities
- Restricted areas

---

13. ART DIRECTION

Iron Requiem uses a dark industrial science-fiction aesthetic.

The visual style prioritizes:

Atmosphere > Visual Complexity

Assets should be optimized for mobile hardware while maintaining strong visual identity.

Important characteristics:

- Grunge textures
- Industrial materials
- Controlled lighting
- Strong silhouettes
- Fog and atmospheric effects
- Practical environmental details
- Horror-focused composition

---

14. AUDIO DESIGN

Sound is extremely important to the horror experience.

The game should use:

- Industrial ambience
- Mechanical noises
- Distant sounds
- Electrical interference
- Metal impacts
- Footsteps
- Environmental noises
- Sudden silence
- Distorted sounds
- Chase music

Music should not constantly play.

Silence is an important part of the experience.

---

15. HORROR STYLE

Iron Requiem combines several forms of horror.

Psychological Horror

Fear created through uncertainty, isolation and anticipation.

Sci-Fi Horror

Robotics, artificial intelligence, technology and abandoned industrial systems.

Body Horror

Disturbing mechanical and biological imagery.

Environmental Horror

The environment itself communicates that something terrible happened.

Pursuit Horror

Chases and encounters where the player must escape rather than fight.

17. MOBILE OPTIMIZATION

Iron Requiem is designed specifically with mobile hardware in mind.

Optimization priorities include:

- Efficient 3D models
- Controlled polygon counts
- Optimized textures
- Occlusion
- Level streaming where appropriate
- Efficient lighting
- Limited expensive effects
- Careful use of particles
- Efficient AI
- Mobile-friendly shaders

Visual quality should be balanced against performance.

21. RECRUITMENT

Nexyron Studios does not automatically accept everyone who applies.

The recruitment process is based on the following steps:

Step 1 — Portfolio

The candidate presents previous projects or work relevant to their role.

Examples:

3D Modeler

- Models
- Environments
- Characters
- Props

Programmer

- Previous games
- Systems
- Tools
- Code samples

Artist

- Concept art
- Illustrations
- Textures

Composer

- Previous music
- Soundtracks
- Audio projects

Step 2 — Discussion

The team discusses:

- Experience
- Skills
- Previous work
- Availability
- Interests
- Expectations

Step 3 — Project Discussion

The candidate receives information about Iron Requiem relevant to their potential role.

Step 4 — Mutual Decision

Joining Nexyron Studios is a mutual decision.

The studio evaluates the candidate, but the candidate also decides whether they want to work on the project.

Step 5 — Joining

If both sides agree, the candidate officially joins the project.

22. WORK MODEL

Iron Requiem is currently an independent project without a conventional development budget.

The project operates under a RevShare / Profit Sharing model.

This means contributors should understand the financial situation of the project before joining.

Any future revenue-sharing agreements should be clearly discussed with contributors before work begins.

25. PROJECT GOAL

The goal of Iron Requiem is to create a memorable mobile horror experience that combines:

Atmosphere + Mystery + Exploration + Psychological Horror + Sci-Fi + Survival

The project should feel like more than a simple mobile horror game.

The goal is to create a world that makes the player curious enough to keep exploring — even when they know they probably shouldn't.
