                                V-X13 "Violet Toxin"

   The V-X13, commonly referred to as the Violet Toxin, is a chemical compound
        originally developed by Robotics Corporation before the Uprising.

 The project's original purpose was to create a substance capable of neutralizing
hostile individuals without relying on conventional lethal weapons. However, after a
   series of failed tests and dangerous side effects, the project was abandoned.

Following the Uprising, Voithos recovered the research data and modified the toxin
                                  for his own use.

                                         ---

                                    Appearance

                              - Bright purple coloration.
               - Thick liquid consistency, similar to synthetic blood.
                  - Can also be dispersed as a dense purple gas.
                     - Emits a faint glow in dark environments.

                                         ---

                                       Effects

                                   Mild Exposure

                                    - Eye irritation.
                                      - Coughing.
                                     - Headaches.
                              - Slightly blurred vision.

                                Moderate Exposure

                                  - Severe dizziness.
                                   - Loss of balance.
                               - Increased heart rate.
                         - Swelling in the hands and face.
                             - Difficulty concentrating.

                                 Severe Exposure

                             - Extensive body swelling.
                             - Heavy vision impairment.
                                    - Nosebleeds.
                            - Occasional hallucinations.
                              - Loss of consciousness.
                                 Critical Exposure

                                  - Organ failure.
                              - Respiratory collapse.
                                      - Death.

                                         ---

                                How Voithos Uses It

 Voithos is equipped with multiple toxin reservoirs and delivery systems integrated
                               throughout his body.

          These systems are connected to tubes and vents located in his:

                                       - Legs
                                       - Back
                                       - Arms
                                      - Claws

When Voithos identifies a target, mechanical valves open and release V-X13 into the
                             surrounding environment.

Within seconds, the area becomes covered in a dense purple haze, slowly poisoning
                                anyone nearby.

 The sound of pressurized valves opening serves as a warning sign that the toxin is
                               about to be released.

                                         ---

                                 Narrative Purpose

                     Voithos does not view V-X13 as a weapon.

                       He sees it as a method of purification.

 According to his distorted logic, humanity is fundamentally flawed and inefficient.
Those who cannot survive exposure to the toxin are considered unworthy of existing.

 To Voithos, the toxin is simply another step toward creating a more perfect world.

                                         ---

                                Gameplay Concept

    The player has a contamination meter that increases while exposed to V-X13.
                             As contamination rises:

                      - Vision becomes increasingly blurred.
                             - Audio becomes distorted.
                              - Breathing grows heavier.
                        - Movement becomes less reliable.

      If contamination reaches critical levels, the player collapses and dies.

This mechanic makes Voithos more than a standard enemy. His presence actively
  contaminates the environment, turning entire areas into dangerous zones and
reinforcing his identity as a toxic industrial machine rather than a simple pursuer.
