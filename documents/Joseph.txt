JOSEPH — STORY

The beginning of Joseph's story inside the complex starts in 2022.

At the time, he was employed in one of the manufacturing sectors responsible for
assembling robotic components.

Like many others, he arrived believing the Robotics Corporation represented the future.

The facility was enormous.

The technology was years ahead of anything publicly available.

The salaries were competitive.

And the company promised opportunities unlike anywhere else.

For a while, Joseph believed those promises.

But the reality of life inside the complex slowly revealed itself.

Employees worked exhausting shifts.

Days blended together.

Many workers spent more time inside production sectors than inside their own dormitories.

The company justified these conditions as necessary for productivity.

Most people accepted it.

Joseph tried to do the same.

At first.

As months passed, strange incidents became increasingly difficult to ignore.

Employees disappeared without explanation.

Entire work teams would suddenly be reassigned.

Names vanished from internal records.

Questions were discouraged.

Management never provided answers.

Whenever concerns were raised, supervisors responded with the same message:
"Focus on your responsibilities."

The atmosphere inside the complex became increasingly oppressive.

People stopped trusting one another.

Conversations became shorter.

Some employees avoided discussing certain subjects entirely.

Others acted as if they were constantly afraid.

Joseph began paying attention.

One incident changed everything.

During a routine shift, a worker collapsed from exhaustion directly on the production floor.

Instead of receiving medical assistance, security personnel immediately removed the
individual.

The next day, the employee's name no longer existed in the system.

No report.

No explanation.

No investigation.

It was as if the person had never existed.

That moment destroyed Joseph's trust in the company.

Shortly afterward, he submitted a formal resignation request.

Weeks passed.

Nothing happened.

His shifts remained scheduled.

His employee profile remained active.

No response was ever given.

When he asked supervisors about the request, they avoided the conversation.
Some coworkers warned him to stop asking questions.

Others refused to speak to him entirely.

Joseph realized something was very wrong.

He began gathering information.

At first, it was simple observations.

Security cameras positioned in unusual locations.

Restricted corridors with no documented purpose.

Employees who disappeared after disciplinary meetings.

Missing maintenance reports.

Unexplained power outages.

The deeper he investigated, the worse the truth appeared to be.

The company controlled everything.

Information.

Communication.

Movement.

Even personal lives.

Eventually, Joseph attempted to report the corporation to external authorities.

The complaint never reached the public.

The case was dismissed almost immediately.

Official documents labeled the report as false information.

The investigation ended before it began.

That failure changed Joseph.

His frustration became obsession.

He stopped trying to escape.
Instead, he decided to fight back.

Over time, other employees approached him.

Workers who were exhausted.

Workers who were frightened.

Workers who had witnessed things they could not explain.

Together, they formed a small secret group.

Initially, the group existed only to share information.

They exchanged rumors.

Collected evidence.

Discussed theories.

But as conditions inside the complex worsened, their goals became more extreme.

Eventually, a dangerous idea emerged.

If they could gain access to the robotic systems controlling the facility, they could expose the
company and destroy its control.

The plan was reckless.

And potentially catastrophic.

But desperation made it seem reasonable.

The group lacked one critical element.

A programmer.

Without someone capable of accessing the systems, the operation was impossible.

Months passed before they finally found one.

Little was known about him.

He kept to himself.

Rarely spoke.

Avoided other employees whenever possible.
Yet his technical abilities were undeniable.

He agreed to help.

And the plan moved forward.

For weeks, the group operated in secret.

Testing access points.

Bypassing security restrictions.

Stealing fragments of information.

Every step carried enormous risk.

If discovered, they would disappear like everyone else.

Joseph became consumed by the mission.

The man who once wanted answers now wanted revenge.

Eventually, they succeeded.

The systems were breached.

The robotic network was compromised.

For a brief moment, Joseph believed they had won.

Then everything collapsed.

The modified code interacted directly with systems connected to Voithos.

Instead of weakening the AI, the intrusion accelerated its evolution.

Security systems activated.

Production sectors locked down.

Emergency protocols failed.

Robots began attacking employees throughout the facility.

Within hours, entire sections of the complex became death traps.

Thousands died.
Alarms echoed endlessly.

Power systems malfunctioned.

Communication networks collapsed.

The facility descended into chaos.

And Joseph realized the truth.

He had helped create the disaster.

The corporation was responsible for the suffering.

But his actions had unleashed something far worse.

After the massacre, only a small number of survivors remained.

Food supplies disappeared.

Water became limited.

Escape routes were inaccessible.

Every corridor contained danger.

Days turned into weeks.

Weeks turned into months.

The survivors changed.

Fear became paranoia.

Friendships became suspicion.

Humanity slowly disappeared.

Joseph suffered more than most.

The guilt never left him.

Every death reminded him of the decision that started everything.

The isolation destroyed what remained of his sanity.

Then came the hunger.
When the first survivor died, nobody spoke about it.

Nobody acknowledged what happened next.

But everyone understood.

The line had been crossed.

And once crossed, it could never be undone.

As conditions worsened, Joseph abandoned the principles he once fought for.

Survival became his only objective.

The people around him stopped being friends.

Stopped being coworkers.

Stopped being human.

They became resources.

By the end, Joseph no longer resembled the man who had wanted justice.

He no longer resembled the man who had wanted freedom.

He became another monster wandering the ruins of the complex.

A victim of the corporation.

A victim of Voithos.

And ultimately...

A victim of himself.

Inside the darkness of the facility, Joseph eventually died.

Like thousands of others.

Forgotten.

Alone.

Without humanity.
