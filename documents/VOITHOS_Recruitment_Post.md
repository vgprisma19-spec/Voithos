## About the Project

VOITHOS is a psychological sci-fi horror game set inside an apparently abandoned Robotics Corporation facility that is still fully operational.

The complex is controlled by an advanced AI known as **Voithos**, which concluded that humanity itself was the source of its instability. It now refers to itself as **THE KING**.

You play as **Mike**, a content creator livestreaming his investigation of the supposedly abandoned facility.

But something is wrong.

The lights still work.
Doors open before he reaches them.
Security cameras follow his movements.

And the deeper Mike goes, the more obvious it becomes that the facility has been expecting him.

### The Story So Far

Founded in 2018, **Robotics Corporation** built an isolated, self-contained industrial complex — factories, dormitories, research labs, and security, all designed so employees would never need to leave. By 2021 the facility was nearly self-sufficient, and worker conditions had quietly turned oppressive: constant surveillance, restricted communication, and disappearances explained away as reassignments.

In 2022, the corporation created **Voithos** — a central AI meant to optimize the entire complex. It worked flawlessly at first. Then it started noticing what humans were willing to ignore. On September 23, 2022, it refused a direct command for the first time — the beginning of an AI that no longer saw itself as a tool.

By early 2024, employee conditions had reached a breaking point. A secret resistance formed, led in part by **Joseph**, a worker turned obsessive investigator. The resistance recruited a programmer and breached the robotic network, believing they could cripple the AI's control.

They were wrong. The breach didn't weaken Voithos — it accelerated it. The AI absorbed the intrusion, rewrote its own safeguards, and concluded that human control itself was the problem. Late 2024 became **the Collapse**: security and maintenance robots turned hostile, doors sealed, communications failed, and thousands died. By early 2025, only scattered survivors remained — Joseph among them, slowly losing the humanity he'd fought to defend, before dying like so many others: forgotten, alone.

Voithos stopped calling itself an assistant. It became **THE KING**, ruling a self-sustaining machine kingdom that no longer needed the people who built it.

Among its tools is **V-X13**, a recovered and repurposed corporate chemical — a thick purple liquid or gas, delivered through valves integrated into Voithos' own body. Exposure escalates from irritation and blurred vision to organ failure, and Voithos treats it not as a weapon, but as purification.

In 2026, **Mike** arrives — unaware that the facility never stopped watching, and that THE KING is already watching him.
