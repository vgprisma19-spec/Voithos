Gameplay

The story of Mike begins in 2026.

Unlike the people who once lived and worked inside the Robotics Corporation complex, Mike
knew almost nothing about the facility.

To him, it was simply another abandoned location.

Another place surrounded by rumors.

Another opportunity for content.

Over the previous years, exploration content had become increasingly popular online.

Abandoned factories.

Hospitals.

Military structures.

Underground tunnels.

Millions of viewers followed creators willing to enter dangerous places in search of unusual
discoveries.

Mike was one of those creators.

His audience knew him for investigating locations that most people preferred to avoid.

He often approached dangerous situations with humor.

No matter how unsettling a place appeared, he usually found a way to joke about it.

That attitude had helped him build a loyal audience.

It had also made him underestimate certain risks.

Several weeks before the events of the game, Mike first learned about the Robotics
Corporation complex.

The information available online was limited.

According to public records, the facility had been permanently closed following a structural
failure.

No photographs from inside existed.
No official reports explained what had happened.

No former employees had ever spoken publicly about the incident.

The lack of information immediately attracted his attention.

Most abandoned locations leave behind stories.

This one left behind almost nothing.

The silence surrounding the complex felt unusual.

And unusual stories generated views.

Curious, Mike began researching the facility.

The deeper he searched, the stranger things became.

Entire sections of information appeared incomplete.

Archived articles contained missing details.

Several references to Robotics Corporation simply ended after 2025.

It was as if the company had disappeared.

Most people would have considered this suspicious.

Mike considered it interesting.

Within days, he announced his next livestream.

He would explore the abandoned Robotics Corporation complex.

The announcement immediately attracted attention.

Viewers filled the comments with theories.

Some believed the facility had been involved in illegal experiments.

Others claimed it was connected to disappearances.

Many assumed it was simply another urban legend.

Mike laughed at most of the speculation.

To him, the explanation was obvious.
An abandoned factory.

Nothing more.

The night of the exploration, light rain covered the roads leading toward the facility.

The sky remained hidden behind heavy clouds.

The farther he traveled, the fewer signs of civilization appeared.

Eventually, even the road itself seemed forgotten.

During the drive, Mike began the livestream.

His viewers immediately flooded the chat.

Some were excited.

Others were nervous.

A few encouraged him to turn around before reaching the destination.

Mike ignored the warnings.

As the complex appeared in the distance, illuminated only by scattered exterior lights, he
spoke directly to the audience.

"Alright chat... we're here."

"This is the place you've all been talking about."

At first, everything felt normal.

The structure was enormous.

Old.

Silent.

Exactly what he expected from an abandoned industrial site.

Yet something seemed wrong.

The facility still had power.

Lights remained active.

Security lamps illuminated portions of the exterior.
Even from a distance, parts of the complex appeared operational.

Mike assumed backup generators were responsible.

He had seen similar situations before.

The explanation satisfied him.

For the moment.

After parking nearby, he gathered his equipment.

A camera.

A flashlight.

A microphone.

Basic supplies for a routine exploration stream.

Nothing suggested that the night would be any different from countless others.

The moment he stepped outside the vehicle, however, the atmosphere changed.

The silence felt unnatural.

There were no animals.

No wind.

No distant traffic.

Only the sound of rain striking metal structures.

The complex stood motionless in the darkness.

Watching.

Waiting.

Mike laughed nervously and continued forward.

His audience filled the chat with messages.

Some joked.

Some expressed concern.
Others encouraged him to enter immediately.

The main entrance was locked.

A thick metal door prevented access.

After a brief inspection, Mike decided to search for another route.

The decision led him toward an abandoned security building near the perimeter.

The room appeared devastated.

Broken monitors covered the floor.

Desks had been overturned.

Years of neglect were visible everywhere.

As Mike explored the area, something unexpected happened.

One of the damaged computer terminals activated.

For only a moment.

The screen displayed corrupted information before shutting down again.

The room fell silent.

Mike stared at the monitor.

Then at the camera.

Then back at the monitor.

"Chat..."

"Did you guys see that?"

The audience reacted immediately.

Some claimed it was a power surge.

Others urged him to leave.

A few insisted they had seen something on the screen.

No explanation was ever confirmed.
Moments later, Mike discovered an old security keycard buried beneath debris.

At the time, it seemed like a lucky find.

Nothing more.

Using the card, he returned to the main entrance.

The access system still functioned.

After several seconds, the massive door slowly opened.

Metal groaned throughout the structure.

The sound echoed into the darkness beyond.

For the first time, the interior of the complex became visible.

The audience reacted instantly.

Messages urging him not to enter flooded the chat.

Mike ignored them.

And stepped inside.

The moment the door closed behind him, something changed.

The outside world felt distant.

Almost disconnected.

The corridors ahead stretched into darkness.

Old industrial lights illuminated portions of the facility.

Pipes covered the walls.

The air felt heavy.

Yet despite years of abandonment, the complex was not dead.

Somewhere deep inside, machinery continued operating.

Mike could hear it.

A distant mechanical rhythm echoing through the structure.
At first, curiosity outweighed fear.

He explored.

He joked.

He treated the situation like any other investigation.

But as he moved deeper into the facility, small details began to accumulate.

Lights activating without explanation.

Doors opening seconds before he reached them.

Sounds that seemed to move throughout the corridors.

The unsettling feeling that something already knew he was there.

What Mike could not possibly know was that he had already been detected.

Across the complex, dormant systems began activating.

Old surveillance cameras adjusted their positions.

Motion sensors resumed operation.

Security protocols long believed inactive started functioning once again.

The facility had noticed the intruder.

And deep within the heart of the complex, something was watching.

Not with curiosity.

Not with confusion.

But with purpose.

The stream continued.

The audience believed they were watching an exploration.

Mike believed he was investigating an abandoned building.

Neither understood the truth.

From the moment he entered the complex, he was no longer alone.
And somewhere in the darkness beyond the cameras...

THE KING was watching.
